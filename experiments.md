# Experiment log (for the report)

All numbers below were produced by the code in this repository. Nothing here is estimated.

## 1. Detector training (image level)

YOLO11 detectors fine-tuned from COCO weights on D-Fire (train 15,471 images, val 1,750, official test 4,306).
Settings: 40 epochs, 640 px, seed 42, patience 15, Ultralytics 8.4.212, Tesla T4 (Colab),
extra CCTV augmentation (ToGray, blur, JPEG compression, Gaussian noise, CLAHE).
Our validation split is carved from D-Fire train, keeping blocks of 50 consecutively numbered
images together so near-duplicate video frames do not leak between train and val.

| Model | Val mAP50 | Val mAP50-95 | Test P | Test R | Test mAP50 | Test mAP50-95 | Test mAP50 smoke | Test mAP50 fire | Inference (T4) | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| YOLO11n | 0.660 | 0.372 | 0.758 | 0.682 | 0.752 | 0.430 | 0.813 | 0.690 | 2.4 ms | 5.5 MB |
| YOLO11s | 0.668 | 0.379 | 0.763 | 0.689 | 0.764 | 0.437 | 0.823 | 0.705 | 5.5 ms | 19 MB |

Test = official D-Fire test split (4,302 images used; Ultralytics ignored 4 images whose labels
have out-of-bounds coordinates).
These numbers come from the Colab T4 evaluation shown in `notebooks/`. Re-running the same
evaluation on the Mac (`tools/reproduce_results.sh`, device mps) gives values within about 0.004
(for example YOLO11n test mAP50 0.751); those runs are the files in `docs/image_eval/`.

## 2. Video data

D-Fire surveillance videos: VP1 to VP50 contain early, distant wildfire smoke (30 s, 2 fps);
FP1 to FP50 are normal scenes chosen to resemble smoke or fire (fog, low cloud, mist from a car
wash, headlights, sun glare, raindrops; 1 to 30 fps). We use the authors' official split:
`video_valid.txt` (15 VP + 15 FP) for all tuning, `video_test.txt` (35 VP + 35 FP) only for final
reporting. Annotations: `clips/dfire_videos/annotations_valid.csv`, `annotations_test.csv`.
Since VP clips show smoke from the first frame, delay is measured from the start of the clip.

Metrics: a VP video is "caught" if at least one smoke alarm is raised. Every alarm on an FP video,
every fire alarm on a VP video and every repeated smoke alarm within one VP video counts as a false alarm.

## 3. Tuning on validation videos (YOLO11n)

Grid over smoke confidence, window length, positive ratio, minimum positive frames,
clear time, motion check and a "large box needs higher confidence" rule (3,192 settings,
replayed from one cached low-threshold inference run with `tools/evaluate_videos.py`).

Findings on validation:
* Initial untuned settings: 10/15 caught, 4 false alarms.
* Most false alarms were fog or low cloud detected as large smoke boxes. In VP videos all smoke
  boxes were under 3% of the frame; on FP videos smoke boxes had a median of 38% of the frame.
* A hard cap on box size was rejected: 41% of smoke images in the D-Fire test set have a smoke box
  covering more than 25% of the frame. Instead large boxes (>= 25% of frame) must reach
  confidence 0.65 (on 40 test images with large real smoke, the best large smoke box had a median
  confidence of 0.78 and 83% reached 0.65; smoke boxes in the validation FP videos had a median of 0.33).
* The motion check gave no benefit: fog moves more than distant smoke. Left disabled.
* Chosen setting (middle of a stable region): smoke confidence 0.25, window 2 s, 60% of frames
  positive, at least 3 positive frames, clear after 8 s, large-box rule 0.25 / 0.65.
  Validation (cloud CPU run): 12/15 caught, 0 false alarms, median 2.75 s to alarm.

## 4. Video-level results (config frozen before the test videos were run)

Official numbers are the ones regenerated by `bash tools/reproduce_results.sh mps` on an Apple
Silicon MacBook Air (tables in `docs/video_eval/`). A first run on a cloud CPU gave the same
conclusions with small differences (for example 0 instead of 1 validation false alarm for YOLO11n),
because detections differ slightly between CPU and MPS arithmetic near the confidence thresholds.

Validation (30 videos, 13.5 min):

| Variant | Model | Smoke videos caught | Normal videos with an alarm | False alarms (fire/smoke) | Per hour | Median delay |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frame-wise (alarm on any detection) | 11n | 12/15 | 4/15 | 6 (1/5) | 26.8 | 0.25 s |
| Temporal, no large-box rule | 11n | 12/15 | 3/15 | 3 (0/3) | 13.4 | 3.25 s |
| Final config | 11n | 12/15 | 1/15 | 1 (0/1) | 4.5 | 3.25 s |
| Frame-wise (alarm on any detection) | 11s | 13/15 | 6/15 | 9 (2/7) | 40.1 | 0.5 s |
| Temporal, no large-box rule | 11s | 13/15 | 5/15 | 6 (1/5) | 26.8 | 2.0 s |
| Final config | 11s | 13/15 | 3/15 | 4 (1/3) | 17.8 | 2.0 s |

YOLO11n was selected on validation (1 false alarm against 4, one fewer smoke video caught).

Test (70 videos, 31.4 min):

| Variant | Model | Smoke videos caught | Normal videos with an alarm | False alarms (fire/smoke) | Per hour | Median delay |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frame-wise (alarm on any detection) | 11n | 31/35 | 17/35 | 23 (6/17) | 44.0 | 0.0 s |
| Temporal, no large-box rule | 11n | 30/35 | 12/35 | 13 (3/10) | 24.9 | 1.25 s |
| **Final config (submitted)** | **11n** | **30/35** | **12/35** | **13 (3/10)** | **24.9** | **1.25 s** |
| Frame-wise (alarm on any detection) | 11s | 31/35 | 17/35 | 23 (4/19) | 44.0 | 1.0 s |
| Temporal, no large-box rule | 11s | 31/35 | 12/35 | 15 (3/12) | 28.7 | 2.0 s |
| Final config | 11s | 31/35 | 8/35 | 11 (3/8) | 21.0 | 2.0 s |

Observations:
* Temporal verification cut false alarms by 43% for YOLO11n (23 to 13) at the cost of one missed
  smoke video, and by 35% for YOLO11s (23 to 15) with no missed video.
* The large-box rule helped YOLO11s on both splits (5 to 3 and 12 to 8 normal videos with an alarm)
  and YOLO11n on validation (3 to 1), but made no difference for YOLO11n on test.
* Validation (15 normal clips) underestimated test false alarms (1/15 against 12/35). YOLO11s did
  better on test, but switching models after seeing test results would be test-set tuning, so this
  is reported as an observation only.
* Remaining false alarms: fog or low cloud with high detector confidence (FP34, FP38), mist and
  headlights (FP40), a small bright orange spot (FP29), vehicle lights on a night street (FP31). Missed smoke: very faint, distant plumes
  (VP5, VP7, VP9, VP13, VP41) that the detector never or rarely detects.
* Speed: all 100 videos (13,609 frames read, 6,432 processed) in 116 s for YOLO11n and 164 s for
  YOLO11s with `--device mps` on the MacBook Air.

## 5. Team recordings (handheld phone video, indoor and outdoor)

Five clips in `clips/own/`, run with the frozen config (outputs in `results/own/`). Boxes were
checked by eye, because an alarm can be raised for the wrong reason.

| Clip | Content | YOLO11n (submitted) | YOLO11s |
|---|---|---|---|
| lamp_fire (44 s) | oil lamp lit at about 5 s, held in hand | correct fire alarm at 6.4 s (onset estimate 5.2 s); smoke alarm at 38 s when the wick smoked | fire 6.2 s; smoke 38.2 s |
| paper_fire_smoke (95 s) | crumpled paper lit on the floor at about 6 s | correct fire alarm at 7.6 s (onset estimate 6.4 s), fire boxed in 433 of 476 frames; smoke alarm at 69.8 s with a box covering the whole frame (haze is visible, but the location is imprecise) | fire 7.8 s; smoke 26.6 s and again 86.4 s |
| garden_view, mountain_view, sea_view | outdoor scenery, low cloud over hills | no alarm | no alarm |

On our recordings YOLO11n raised a correct fire alarm for both fire clips about 1.2 s after
ignition and raised no false alarm on the normal clips.
Small flames, thin indoor smoke and orange objects are the main limitations to expect on new
indoor footage, since D-Fire is mostly outdoor fires and large plumes.
The verification example (`examples/`, `expected_outputs/`) uses a 16 s cut of lamp_fire and the
full mountain_view clip, re-encoded to 1280x720 at 30 fps.
